# What you will explore

Event-driven cameras adoption in real-world applications is massively growing, thanks to their low-power, low latency, high temporal resolution, high dynamic range and highly compressive encoding. The EVS school is the first event focused on **teaching computer vision with event cameras**. Its aim is to offer students an in-depth knowledge of state-of-the-art methods to process event-driven camera data and teach them the required, practical, skills to develop their own applications.

In addition to lectures from leading international researchers, participants will be involved in hands-on projects and group work with sufficient time to build practical skills in processing event camera data. **All hands-on and group work will be deployable live on event cameras during the school to ensure full pipeline development, from conceptualisation to live demos!** The program is complemented with keynote talks on event-driven latest research and industrial applications.
